Privacy notice

This notice explains how PhoennixAI handles the personal information collected through the Kaizen OS early-access waitlist.

Last updated: 23 September 2026 · Launch draft

1. What we collect

When you join the Kaizen OS waitlist, we collect the information you choose to provide in the form. Required details are your first name and email address. Company or organisation and role are optional. We record whether you accepted the waitlist terms and whether you opted in to optional product news.

We collect only what the waitlist needs.Joining does not require sensitive personal information. Please do not submit it through the form.

2. Why we use your information

We use the information to manage interest in early access, send a confirmation that the request was received, send an invitation when access is available, understand at a high level who is interested in the product when optional details are supplied, and respond to requests about the waitlist.

Optional product-news emails are separate from operational confirmation and invitation emails.

3. Lawful basis

Before launch, PhoennixAI must document the lawful basis for each processing purpose. Where direct marketing rules require consent, the optional marketing preference must be kept separate, clear and recorded. Operational messages connected with a waitlist request are handled separately from optional marketing.

4. Who may receive your information

Access is limited to authorised PhoennixAI operators and service providers required to run the waitlist. The initial implementation uses Microsoft 365 services: Excel Online stores the waitlist, Power Automate handles the workflows, and Outlook sends operational emails.

5. How long we keep it

Waitlist information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to manage the Kaizen OS early-access programme, communicate with people who joined it and maintain appropriate records. It is then deleted or anonymised unless a longer period is required for a specific legal or operational reason.

Before launch, replace this criterion with the final retention period or a more specific retention rule.

6. Your rights

Depending on the circumstances and the lawful basis used, you may have rights including access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection and data portability. You can ask to be removed from the waitlist. Where processing is based on consent, you can withdraw it.

7. Data security

PhoennixAI should apply appropriate technical and organisational controls to protect waitlist information from unauthorised access, loss, misuse and disclosure. Workbook access must be restricted to the people who operate the programme.

8. Contact and complaints

For privacy questions, access requests or removal requests, use the PhoennixAI privacy contact published on the production site. If you are unhappy with how personal information is handled, you may also raise a concern with the relevant data-protection authority.

Before launch, replace this placeholder with the final legal entity name, postal address and privacy contact details, and obtain appropriate legal review.

9. Changes to this notice

We may update this notice as the waitlist and supporting systems develop. The current version is published on this page with its updated date.
